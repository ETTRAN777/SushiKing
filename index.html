Our Heritage

Crafting The Legend

Located in the heart of Rochester Hills, Sushi King brings together artisanal precision and contemporary culinary art. Every plate is a masterclass in balance, utilizing premium fresh selections sourced globally to deliver a royal dining experience.

Our master chefs treat sushi as a discipline of absolute refinement. From the meticulous temperature calibration of our seasoned rice to the master-level knife cuts of pristine catches, we preserve traditional heritage while innovating bold, signature sensory profiles.
